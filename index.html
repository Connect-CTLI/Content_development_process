PDD Course Design Hub

Choose the programme team and add the course draft. The Hub reviews it against UDL and the Moodle course flow for you to approve, then snippets the draft into resources, fills in the Resource Creation, Question Bank, Snippets and Design Blueprint tabs, and gives you the sheet back to download. Once the developers have chosen their elements, it makes each developer a pack of their content. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

2 Add the original course draft

The draft is checked against Universal Design for Learning (UDL) and the CTLI Moodle course flow. It must use the Course Draft template: Heading 1 for each module (starting "Module"), Heading 2 for each topic, and bold lines for activities such as "Assignment:", "Reflective post:" or "Knowledge check:".

3 Learning design review

Each suggestion shows the original on the left and the suggested change on the right. Download the reviewed draft to see them in Word: added sections are tracked changes highlighted in yellow, and advice is in comments in the margin. Accept or reject each one, then add the approved draft in step 4.

Add a course draft in step 2.

What the review checks
  • Engagement (why we learn): a welcome and relevance to participants' schools, knowledge checks with feedback, reflection.
  • Representation (what we learn): more than one way to take in key content, chunked topics, short sentences and paragraphs, acronyms written out, key takeaways.
  • Action & Expression (how we show learning): observable objectives, time to complete, a choice of format for assignments, success criteria.
  • Accessibility: descriptive link text, alt text on images, tables introduced and with header rows.
  • Moodle flow: the six course page tabs, then each module as overview, content, activity, knowledge check, reflection and key takeaways, and a course feedback survey at the end.

6 Check the snippets, then confirm

Each row is one resource with its snippet of content. Untick anything that should not go into the sheet. The type is what the draft already contains (a reading page, a Moodle activity, a table, a link). Suggested elements are ideas for the developers only: they choose what to build in the Resource Creation Sheet.

7 Download

Download the filled sheet and share it with the developers. They choose their elements in Resource Creation (Visual Instructional Designer, digital tools and product type) and check the Duration to develop (hh:mm) for each: it starts at a typical duration for the item type, and they can change it. The Project Plan tab fills itself as the tracker is updated: complete it and export it to PDF when the project closes.
