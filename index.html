Course Draft Sorter

Add the course draft and the Resource Creation Sheet (T06). The sorter snippets the draft into resources, fills in the Resource Creation, Question Bank, Snippets and Design Blueprint tabs, and gives you the sheet back to download. Once the developers have chosen their elements, it makes each developer a pack of their content. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

1 Add the course draft and the sheet

The draft must use the Course Draft template: Heading 1 for each module (starting "Module"), Heading 2 for each topic, and bold lines for activities such as "Assignment:", "Reflective post:" or "Knowledge check:".

COURSE DRAFT · .DOCX Drop the course draft here, or click to choose it Word, or Google Docs downloaded as Microsoft Word (.docx)
RESOURCE CREATION SHEET · .XLSX Drop the Resource Creation Sheet (T06) here, or click to choose it Excel, or Google Sheets downloaded as Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)

2 Course set-up

These go into every row the sorter adds. The course code makes the item codes: the programme, the type, then the main source and the source element, exactly as column A of Resource Creation builds them.

Item codes will look like LP3Text_Comp2.1.2

3 Check the snippets, then confirm

Each row is one resource with its snippet of content. Untick anything that should not go into the sheet. The type is what the draft already contains (a reading page, a Moodle activity, a table, a link). Suggested elements are ideas for the developers only: they choose what to build in the Resource Creation Sheet.

4 Download

Download the filled sheet and share it with the developers. They choose their elements in Resource Creation (Visual Instructional Designer, digital tools, VID and product type). The Project Plan tab fills itself as the tracker is updated: complete it and export it to PDF when the project closes.
