Course Draft Sorter

Choose the programme team, add the course draft, and the Resource Creation Sheet (T06) is already loaded. The sorter snippets the draft into resources, fills in the Resource Creation, Question Bank, Snippets and Design Blueprint tabs, and gives you the sheet back to download. Once the developers have chosen their elements, it makes each developer a pack of their content. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

1 Programme team

Choose the unit and its Programme Co-ordinator. They go into the Design Blueprint and Project Plan, and the developer packs.

Edit team lists and co-ordinators

Add, rename or remove people here. Your changes are saved in this browser and written into the Data tab of every sheet the sorter fills or downloads, so the sheet's dropdowns match. To change the starting lists for the whole team, use Copy the lists for GitHub and paste them over teams in the settings.

2 Add the course draft

The draft must use the Course Draft template: Heading 1 for each module (starting "Module"), Heading 2 for each topic, and bold lines for activities such as "Assignment:", "Reflective post:" or "Knowledge check:".

3 Course set-up

These go into every row the sorter adds. The course code makes the item codes: the programme, the type, then the main source and the source element, exactly as column A of Resource Creation builds them.

Item codes will look like LP3Text_Comp2.1.2

4 Check the snippets, then confirm

Each row is one resource with its snippet of content. Untick anything that should not go into the sheet. The type is what the draft already contains (a reading page, a Moodle activity, a table, a link). Suggested elements are ideas for the developers only: they choose what to build in the Resource Creation Sheet.

5 Download

Download the filled sheet and share it with the developers. They choose their elements in Resource Creation (Visual Instructional Designer, digital tools and product type) and check the Duration to develop (days) for each: it starts at a typical duration for the item type, and they can change it. The Project Plan tab fills itself as the tracker is updated: complete it and export it to PDF when the project closes.

When the developers have chosen their elements, add the updated sheet here. Pick a developer to download a pack made for them: a Word document with their elements, due dates, duration, checklist and content, and a copy of the sheet showing only their rows.
